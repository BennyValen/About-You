# Scene 06 — Sailboat over a turquoise lagoon

Frames 1674–2077 (69.75–86.58 s). Reference: `work/ref4/` (4 frames per second); crops: `work/scene_notes/crops/s06.jpg` (10/50/90% frames plus 1:1 crops at the subject).

## Environment and materials
- **Shallows**: pale sandy seabed under clear water (#dff6f0 / #c9f4ed), painterly white strokes and caustic streaks.
- **Channels**: winding deep-turquoise channels (#2595ac / #44a2ae / #2f7e90) with soft banks.
- **Coral heads**: crusty, knobbly coral clusters in **purple, olive, ochre/orange and grey-green** (#7c7e6b / #4f6461 ...), many sizes, larger toward the edges.
- **Sea life**: sea turtles swimming, small blue and orange starfish on the sand.
- **Manta ray**: slate-grey manta (#3e4e5e) gliding up-left of the boat, undulating wings, cephalic fins, whip tail, shadow on the seabed.

## Hero subject
- **Sailboat**: wooden dinghy (#c8955c, planked), pointed bow up, mast and boom, **white sail** heeled out to the **right**. Boat and sail shadows fall on the seabed to the right/lower-right.
- **Sailor**: in **navy/indigo**, hiking out over the left gunwale.
- A **wake** of white bubbles behind the stern.

## Screen position (measured)
boat centre (555, 1180); fixed

## Light
Bright sun from the upper-left; seabed shadows offset to the lower-right.
